AI model pipeline

This page explains the machine-learning component used by the system.

Resume + Job Description
↓
TF-IDF Vectorization
↓
Cosine Similarity + Skill Overlap
↓

Why this model?

TF-IDF keeps the project lightweight, offline-friendly, fast to train, and easy to explain in a viva.

Future improvement

Replace TF-IDF with sentence embeddings to improve semantic matching between related terms and phrases.
